# Virality for a scholarly religious page (Instagram, 2026)

## Ranking signals → what the template does

| Signal (weight) | Mechanism in the Reel |
|---|---|
| Watch time / completion / loops (highest) | 3 s hook with an open loop; hadith revealed clause by clause (viewer must stay to finish the sentence); progress chips (٣/٣); seal held as the peak at ~75 %; 34-frame fade so the restart is seamless. Total 42–48 s keeps completion high for text-only. |
| Sends per reach (strongest non-follower signal) | The sanad verification is the *share argument* for this audience («صحيح السند» is forwarded to friends/family); CTA names the recipient («أرسِلْها لمن يُحبُّهم»); send icon on screen. |
| Saves | Full vocalized hadith + sources on screen; footer «احفظِ المقطعَ لتعودَ إلى السَّند»; caption repeats the text. |
| Likes per reach | Beauty and calm — the paper/typography aesthetic gets reflexive likes from the niche. |
| Originality (since 2026-04-30 reposters are removed from recommendations) | Everything is rendered from the page's own cards; brand assets extracted, audio synthesized, no stock footage, no watermarks. |
| Search / topics | Caption first line = hook with Imam + topic keywords; Reel topics set to أهل البيت / حديث / سند; ≤ 8 niche hashtags. |

## Structure beats (keep the order)

1. **Hook** 0–3.5 s — question or counted condition, highlight word green.
2. **Hadith** — the payoff of the hook arrives early (lead line at ~2 s into the scene), then the rest.
3. **Points / Bayan** — the "value" middle; ≤ 7 s per card; chips create micro-goals.
4. **Sanad** — the trust climax: names tick in, stamps «ثقة», then the seal + boom + ding.
5. **CTA** — sources (credibility) → share line → icons → footer → fade.

## Anti-patterns for this niche (hurt distribution or dignity)

- Rhythmic/pop music under hadith text (comments turn hostile; shares drop).
- Faces, AI "cinematic" scenes of holy figures, flashing red warnings.
- More than one hadith per Reel, or > 10 lines; > 5 points; > 60 s.
- Begging CTAs («تابعونا», «لايك»); emoji; exclamation marks; Latin words.
- Reusing another page's footage or screenshots.

## Cold-start checklist (first 60 minutes)

- Post 19:00–22:00 Baghdad/Riyadh; Thursday–Friday nights peak for religious content.
- Pin the rijal comment; answer the first 10 comments with substance (a source, a page number).
- Share to Story with a «أرسِل لمن…» sticker; ask 3–5 trusted accounts to *send* (not like) the Reel.
- After 24 h read retention: drop at hadith → `plan.py --target 40`; drop at points → merge into `bayan`;
  high completion but low sends → stronger recipient-naming CTA (formula: «أرسِلْها لِـ … حتى …»).

## Series logic

Reels that verify a chain accumulate authority: keep the same seal, same bed maqam, same chip style.
Number series in the caption («سلسلة: من هم شيعتهم؟ ١/٣»), never on the cover.
